04  On realism

It sounds like a keyboard.Not a sound effect.

01

Every key its own

Each key gets a slightly different pitch, under half a semitone, and keeps it.

02

Never the same twice

Several recordings per key, picked at random, so repeats never sound copied.

03

One sound per press

Hold a key and it plays once. Auto-repeat never stacks clicks.

04

The way back up

Real switches make a softer second sound when you let go. So does Clacky.

05

Big keys sound bigger

Space, Enter and Delete use deeper versions of the press.

06

Where it sits

With stereo width up, keys come from where they are on the keyboard, left to right.

04

05  On quiet & privacy

It knows when to stay quiet.And what it never hears.

01

Quiet on calls

Pauses while another app uses the microphone: Zoom, FaceTime, Meet, dictation. macOS 14 or later.

02

Silent where you want

Pick apps, like games, where Clacky stays quiet while they're in front.

03

⌃⌥⌘C anywhere

One shortcut turns Clacky on or off from any app. The Dock says “Off” while it's off.

04

Blind to passwords

macOS blocks it entirely while you type into a password field.

05

Only key codes

It learns which physical key went down or came up, plays a sound, and moves on.

06

Plays where you'll hear it

A monitor with no speakers? Clacky moves to your Mac's speakers and tells you.
