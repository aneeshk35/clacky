# Sound pack credits

Every pack here is built from recordings on Freesound.org, sliced into individual key presses
(and releases, where a clean one exists) and level-matched by Clacky's tooling. Space, Enter and
Delete use slightly slowed-down copies of the presses so the big keys sound deeper.

- **Clicky (Real)**: "qKey.wav" (CC0) by UberBosser, https://freesound.org/people/UberBosser/sounds/421583/ ;
  "button click" (CC0) by rydra_wong, https://freesound.org/people/rydra_wong/sounds/622060/
- **Creamy Linear**: "Keyboard typing sounds: Unidentified Technics keyboard" (CC0), https://freesound.org/people/zrrion/sounds/665075/, by zrrion.
- **Creamy Thock**: "Typing on a rainy 75" (CC0), https://freesound.org/people/Techrul/sounds/815614/, by Techrul.
- **Hako Violet**: "Keyboard Typing 10 (WhiteFox, Mechanical)" (CC0), https://freesound.org/people/grcekh/sounds/546167/ ;
  "Keyboard Typing 9 (WhiteFox, Mechanical)" (CC0), https://freesound.org/people/grcekh/sounds/546166/, by grcekh.
- **MX Blue**: "Typing on a keyboard" (CC0) by SamsterBirdies, https://freesound.org/people/SamsterBirdies/sounds/489424/
- **MX Brown**: "Cherry MX Brown Mechanical Keyboard Sound.mp3" (CC0) by NickTHH, https://freesound.org/people/NickTHH/sounds/432432/
- **MX Clear**: "Mechanical keyboard clicking. Different keys (4)" (CC0) by humi74, https://freesound.org/people/humi74/sounds/412926/
- **MX Red**: "Keyboard clicking typing Cherry Red Mechanical" (CC0) by handygaber, https://freesound.org/people/handygaber/sounds/499773/
- **Thocky**: "Thocky Keyboard Typing (No Spaces)" (CC0), https://freesound.org/people/aliyahb/sounds/869165/, by aliyahb.

CC0 sounds are public domain; they're credited anyway.
